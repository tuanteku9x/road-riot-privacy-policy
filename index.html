Privacy Policy

App: Road Riot 3D

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Solo offline arcade motorcycle action game

This Privacy Policy explains how Road Riot 3D ("the App") handles user information. By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Road Riot 3D is designed as a single-player offline game and does not require account registration. The App does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or home address.

The App also does not include in-game chat, multiplayer accounts, or direct user-to-user communication features.

2. Gameplay Data

The App may store gameplay-related data locally on your device, such as:

  • Best scores
  • Game progress
  • Settings such as audio preferences

This data is stored only on your device and is used solely to support gameplay and improve your experience.

3. Internet Access

The core gameplay of Road Riot 3D is intended to work offline. The App does not require users to create an account or submit personal information in order to play.

4. Third-Party Services

The App itself does not intentionally collect personal information. However, if you download or use the App through third-party platforms such as Google Play, those platforms may collect certain technical or device-related information under their own privacy policies.

Please review the applicable policies of third-party platforms for more information:

  • Google Privacy Policy

5. Advertising

Road Riot 3D does not currently display third-party ads inside the App.

6. Children’s Privacy

Road Riot 3D is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that personal information has been provided unintentionally, please contact us so that we can review the issue.

7. Data Security

Because the App does not directly collect personal information, the privacy risk is limited. Any local gameplay data stored on your device remains under your device’s own security controls.

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the “Last updated” date will be revised accordingly.
